# UI/UX sweep — 2026-10-24 (#4)

Walk of the BitSov web shell against `de8b4a9` (current `main`), starting from the 2026-10-23 report. That report walked `321f437`. This pass reads the UI diff since then and looks at phone and narrow layouts (overflow, tap size, safe areas) and at empty, loading, error, offline, disabled, and success states.

The change that matters for this angle is the phone shell (U5, `68ade77`): bottom tabs Chats · Wallet · You, the chat list as a page, and the rail only on a wide window. A narrow Mac still keeps the rail. The phone shell is `data-platform` ios or android, not a CSS width. Also in the range: Dynamic Type tokens, iOS safe-area caps, and the owner-approval silent-fail fix. Accessibility, contrast, and copy were the 23 Oct angle and were not re-audited. One check from that angle failed for a token-format reason and is noted under Local check.

Checked in Chrome with an iPhone user agent against the U5 preview (`docs/ui/u5-phone-shell/harness/preview.html`), viewport 390×844 unless a finding says otherwise. Desktop Chrome reports `env(safe-area-inset-*)` as 0. The new rules still include those insets, so a real iPhone adds them on top of the numbers below. The preview sets `data-platform=ios`, so the iOS spacing caps apply (`--s-1` is about 3.76px, `--s-3` about 11.3px, at a 16px root).

Not in this walk: an installed iOS or Android build, a live node, and `assistant.html`.

## Findings

### 1. An incoming call sits on the Chats / Wallet / You tabs

- **Screen:** Phone, incoming call and meeting card
- **Severity:** High
- **What:** `.call-panel` is `position: fixed` at `bottom: max(var(--s-6), env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` (`src/styles.css` line 984), `z-index: 50`. Meeting and room cards share that class. The tab bar is the 44px control plus its own padding (`src/styles.css` line 1818). On the U5 iPhone geometry that bar is about 83px tall once the home-indicator inset is included. Accept and Decline then sit under the tabs. A first call is the moment a new person has to answer, and the answer buttons are the ones covered.
- **Fix:** On an iOS or Android phone shell, while the keyboard has not already removed the tabs, the card’s bottom is the tab bar plus `--s-3`, and its max-height keeps the top safe area (`src/styles.css` lines 1910–1914). Measured at 390×844 with the arrival animation disabled: computed `bottom` 62.82px, card bottom 781, tabs top 792, Accept (44px tall) ending at 765. The card’s right edge is 367, inside the 390px width. With the keyboard open the old rule stays, because the tabs are `display: none`. `docs/qa/screenshots/2026-10-24-call-390.png`.

### 2. Phone Chats shows “+” and hides “Sample data, no node”

- **Screen:** Phone, Chats, empty and populated list
- **Severity:** Medium
- **What:** The list reuses `.rail-foot`. Under 680px that foot is a sticky horizontal strip and `.mode-tag` is `display: none` (`src/styles.css` lines 903 and 1373). Under 480px `.new-track` is 44×44 with `font-size: 0` and a “+” from `::before` (`src/styles.css` lines 1398–1399). The U5 list capture put that button at x=321, width 44, font 0px, below an 852px viewport. The visible name of New disappeared, and the sample-data tag disappeared with it. The accessible name was already “New” (`Shell.tsx` line 2739); that is unchanged. The horizontal strip on a narrow Mac still uses the “+”.
- **Fix:** `.phone-chat-list .rail-foot` is a normal block again. New is full width, at least 44px tall, in `--t-md`, with no “+” pseudo, and the sample tag is shown (`src/styles.css` lines 1879–1898). Measured after: “+ New” is 17px type, 360×44, at x=15 on a 390px width. Danish “+ Ny” is 330×44 on a 360px width. `docs/qa/screenshots/2026-10-24-chats-390.png`.

### 3. Offline Retry and call repair start below the fold

- **Screen:** Phone status row, node offline and unresolved-call repair
- **Severity:** Medium
- **What:** Both render in `.phone-status` (`Shell.tsx` lines 1916–1918), which is capped at `max-height: 45vh` with its own scroll (`src/styles.css` line 1806). `.call-repairs` adds a second scroller at `max-height: 35vh` (line 1511). The U5 preview scrolls the recovery button into view before it measures, which is how the offline capture left the node pill at a negative y. A person who opens the app and cannot reach the node sees a checklist and no Retry until they discover the status row scrolls.
- **Fix:** The nested repairs scroller is turned off inside the status row. The status max-height, only while offline or repair is present, is the viewport minus the top inset, the tab bar, and 72px of the list (`src/styles.css` lines 1921–1927). The offline illustration is 72px wide and Retry is full width. Measured at 390×844 with zero safe area: offline `scrollTop` 0, Retry from y=492 to 537, status pane bottom 661, tabs top 792, button width 360, pill 360×44 at y=7.5. Repair `scrollTop` 0, repairs `max-height: none`, button bottom 347. On a 393×852 iPhone with a 59px top inset and a 34px bottom inset the cap is about 631px and the card is about 661px, so the tail of the card can still scroll. Retry, which ends near 537px, stays on the first screen. Large type can still scroll the status row; that is the leftover, not the first screen. `docs/qa/screenshots/2026-10-24-offline-390.png`.

### 4. “Connected to your node” wraps in a 180px pill

- **Screen:** Phone status row
- **Severity:** Low
- **What:** The phone pill already asks for `max-width: 100%` (`src/styles.css` line 1807). Inside `@media (max-width: 680px)`, `html[data-platform='ios'] .node-pill { max-width: 180px }` (line 1847) wins, because a media query does not add specificity. That 180px cap is the top rail sharing a line with other controls. On the phone the pill is the only control on the row, and the U5 list capture showed it at 180×66 with “Connected to your node” wrapping. `.on-call-pill` is `white-space: nowrap` and could run off the same row on Android.
- **Fix:** `.phone-status .node-pill` is `width: 100%`, and the iOS rule is overridden to `max-width: 100%` (`src/styles.css` lines 1902–1903). Measured pill: 360×44, one line. The on-call pill in that row may wrap (line 1904). Amounts stay `white-space: nowrap`; this does not touch `.sats` or `.money-text`.

### 5. Wallet chips run off a narrow window and an Android phone

- **Screen:** Wallet tabs and the activity filters
- **Severity:** Low
- **What:** iOS already wraps `.wallet-actions`, `.wallet-top`, `.wallet-tabs`, and `.wallet-row` (`src/styles.css` line 1756). Android’s phone shell, and any window at 480px or below, did not. Activity, Channels, Receive, and Send, and the activity filters, are one unwrapped row. Danish Aktivitet / Kanaler / Modtag / Send and Beskeder / Betalinger are longer and leave the screen.
- **Fix:** Those rows wrap under 480px and inside `.cc[data-phone-shell='true']` (`src/styles.css` lines 1931–1936). `.wallet-filters .modes` is included; the iOS rule does not name it. Measured Danish wallet at 320×700: no horizontal overflow.

### 6. A new phone opens on the desktop rail, not the welcome

- **Screen:** Phone, Chats, no contacts
- **Severity:** Medium
- **What:** Welcome home is inside `.track` (`Shell.tsx` line 1996). That section is hidden when the phone shell is on Chats with no open thread (`Shell.tsx` line 1968: `phoneTab() !== 'chats' || track() === null`). The landing page is the rail: “No contacts yet” (line 2648), “This node does not support rooms yet”, Assistants, Browse (Profiles, Porch), then New. The phone tests require Porch, assistants, and New to stay reachable, so this was not rearranged.
- **Fix:** Not changed. When there are no contacts, lead with the welcome actions and put Porch, assistants, and the rooms sentence behind one disclosure.

### 7. Offline is a checklist, and the pill names the same outage differently

- **Screen:** Phone status row, node offline
- **Severity:** Low
- **What:** Finding 3 puts Retry on the first screen. The card is still an illustration, two paragraphs, a last-seen line, and three bullets (`OfflineNode.tsx` lines 25–36). The pill beside it can say “Remote connection unavailable” or “Starting node” (`state.ts` `nodePill`, lines 428–431) while the heading says “Your node is offline”.
- **Fix:** Not changed. Shorten the card, and use one sentence for the outage, with the product owner.

### 8. Danish Chats is the English word

- **Screen:** Phone tabs, Danish
- **Severity:** Low
- **What:** `phoneChats` is “Chats” in Danish (`appCopy.ts` line 849) while Calls is “Opkald”. `tests/phone-shell.test.tsx` expects `['Chats', 'Tegnebog', 'Dig']`.
- **Fix:** Not changed. The string is intentional in that test. A Danish tab label is a copy decision, not a layout fix.

- You is three buttons: Share my card / QR, Profile and settings, Add a device (`Shell.tsx` lines 2161–2165). Same shape as the 22 Oct note that welcome home is three actions. Left as a design choice.
